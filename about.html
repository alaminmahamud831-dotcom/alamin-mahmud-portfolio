MY APPROACH

My workflow combines platform-assisted discovery with manual review, public business sources and structured spreadsheet work. The goal is to make the list relevant, readable and easier for a sales or outreach team to use.

I work from the client’s brief and agreed qualification rules. Where available, I keep source context and make it clear when a field is missing, uncertain or only marked as verified by the source workbook.

How I approach each project

  • Understand the ideal customer profile before collecting records.
  • Prioritize relevant companies and decision-maker roles.
  • Keep source notes and data status visible.
  • Deliver clean, consistently formatted spreadsheets.
