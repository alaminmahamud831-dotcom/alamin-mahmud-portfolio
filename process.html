WORK PROCESS

A transparent four-step workflow

The process is designed to keep the list aligned with your brief and make the final handoff easy to review.

01

Brief & targeting

Confirm industry, geography, ideal customer profile, company filters, decision-maker roles and required fields.

02

Company & contact research

Identify relevant businesses and decision-makers using agreed tools, search methods and public business sources.

03

Review & data checks

Check consistency, remove obvious duplicates, retain source context and label missing, uncertain or source-verified fields.

04

Structured delivery

Deliver the agreed Excel or Google Sheets format with clear columns, useful notes and a transparent status overview.

HANDOFF

Typical deliverables

  • Targeted company and prospect list
  • Decision-maker name and job title
  • Business contact fields with status labels
  • Website, location and source references where available
  • Clean spreadsheet ready for review or CRM import
